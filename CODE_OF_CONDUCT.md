# Code of conduct

Castnet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/AliKotb1). Reports are handled confidentially.
